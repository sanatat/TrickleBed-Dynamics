# Request for original tracer experiment data — draft (not sent)

Subject: Request for Na-gluconate and KCl RTD data from Sales et al. (2005)

Dear Prof. Abreu,

I am developing a research-oriented numerical study of tracer transport, intraparticle diffusion, and adsorption in trickle-bed reactors. I have been studying your paper *Experimental Dynamic Evaluation of Three-Phase Reactors* (Brazilian Journal of Chemical Engineering, 2005, DOI: 10.1590/S0104-66322005000300012), particularly Equation (16) and Figures 5–6.

The paper mentions using a second tracer (KCl) to help estimate liquid holdup and wetting efficiency. Would the original time–concentration measurements (inlet and outlet, preferably including KCl and sodium gluconate), or any digitized supplementary data, still be available for academic comparison? Measurement timing, repetitions, adsorption tests, tracer-injection protocol and calibration details would also be very helpful.

I would clearly cite the paper and acknowledge any shared data, and I would not redistribute non-public measurements without permission.
